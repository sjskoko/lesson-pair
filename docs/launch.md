## English

I built LessonPair to help turn a video you like into English you can actually use. Give your AI one English video, try a short explanation, practice what you couldn't express, and try again. The before/after card compares your two answers—not an AI rewrite presented as your progress.

Open source, optional Notion, a ChatGPT/Codex skill plugin, and a local tool for your own compatible AI endpoint. Captions are needed; when retrieval fails, paste an excerpt. All demo material is fictional.

## 한국어

좋아하는 영어 영상을 보고 나면, 내 말로 설명할 수 있을까요?

LessonPair는 먼저 짧게 답하고, 막힌 표현을 연습하고, 직접 다시 설명하게 안내하는 오픈소스 학습 도구입니다. AI가 멋지게 고친 문장을 내 실력처럼 보여주지 않고, 내가 작성한 두 답변을 비교합니다.

## Distribution notes

Lead with the learner's visible task and a short synthetic demo. Use relevant communities' sharing rules, disclose project ownership, and ask one concrete feedback question. Avoid star exchanges, fabricated testimonials, unsupported “scientifically proven” claims, mass cross-posts, and implying public plugin-directory approval. Stars and search visibility are not guaranteed.
